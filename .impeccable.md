## Design Context

### Users
Friends and visitors voting for Josh’s tattoo, often opening a shared link on a phone. They compare three designs, choose one, and confirm their vote by email.

### Brand Personality
Playful, handmade, irreverent. Keep the existing paper-and-marker character; the creator requested a small tidy-up and confirmed that this style should stay.

### Aesthetic Direction
Cream paper, yellow marker highlights, blue ink, Londrina Solid headlines and Patrick Hand body copy. Improve spacing and readability without a redesign. Support small phones, touch, and desktop keyboards.

### Design Principles
- Put the three drawings first; fit each whole image and offer an obvious full-size view.
- Make the difference between enlarging a drawing and choosing it explicit.
- Keep choice, email confirmation, and private results easy to understand.
- Make touch targets generous and show clear focus, loading, error, and success states.
- Preserve the informal character while keeping the layout calm and consistent.
